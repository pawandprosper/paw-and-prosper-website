Paw & Prosper Named 2026 Best of Summit for Customer Service

By Samantha Quinones · October 20, 2026

I'm honored to share that Paw & Prosper was voted 1st place for Customer Service in the Summit Daily's 2026 Best of Summit awards, and runner-up for Pet-Friendly Business.

Best of Summit is the Summit Daily's readers' choice contest, and it has been running for over a decade. Locals nominate their favorite businesses, then vote, and the business with the most votes in each category wins. That's what makes this one mean so much to me: it came straight from the people in this community.

Thank You, Summit County

To every client, friend and neighbor who took the time to nominate and vote: thank you. Paw & Prosper is a small business, and seeing it next to so many local favorites I love is a big deal.

What Customer Service Means Here

Most of the people I work with didn't get into animal care to learn SEO. They run clinics, shelters, rescues and pet businesses, and their time belongs to the animals and families they serve. So I try to keep marketing simple: plain-language updates, honest answers about what will and won't make a difference, and a clear next step every time. When you work with Paw & Prosper, you work with me.

And the Pet-Friendly Part

Being runner-up for Pet-Friendly Business made me smile. Paw & Prosper exists because of animals. My Lab-Collie mix, Itachi, is the one who inspired it all, and every business I work with cares for pets or the people who love them.
