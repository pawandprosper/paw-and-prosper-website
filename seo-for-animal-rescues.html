Common Questions from Rescue Organizations

We're a foster-based rescue with no physical location. Can you still help with local SEO?

Yes, and this is something we specialize in. Foster-based rescues require a different approach than brick-and-mortar shelters. We build visibility through service-area signals, content strategy, and Google Business Profile setup that doesn't depend on a storefront address.

We rescue a specific breed. Is that a problem for SEO?

The opposite, actually. Breed-specific rescues have a real keyword advantage. "Golden Retriever rescue in Colorado" is a much easier search to win than "dog adoption near me." We build strategies around that specificity.

How do we get more foster families, not just adopters?

Foster recruitment is a content and visibility problem. We help you show up for the searches and questions potential fosters are asking, and we help you tell the story in a way that converts readers into applicants.

What makes Paw & Prosper different from other marketing agencies?

We only work with animal wellness businesses and nonprofits. We understand rescue operations, the emotional journey of adopters and fosters, and the challenge of standing out when you're running on a shoestring. That context is in everything we build.
