Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Paw & Prosper ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit pawandprosper.com or submit a request for our services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, business name, and website URL when you complete the free audit request form. We do not sell this information to third parties.

We also automatically collect certain information when you visit our website through cookies and standard web analytics tools, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This information is used in aggregate to understand how visitors use our website and improve our content.

Google Services

Our website uses Google Analytics and may use Google Ads to understand traffic patterns and reach potential clients. These services use cookies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites. The data collected helps us understand which of our pages and content are most useful and may be used to show you relevant advertising on other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember your preferences and understand how you interact with the site. We use cookies for:

  • Analytics (Google Analytics) to understand site usage
  • Advertising measurement (Google Ads) to understand which channels bring visitors to our site
  • Form functionality via our third-party form provider, Jotform

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality on this site.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services on this website:

  • Jotform -- processes audit request form submissions. Jotform's privacy policy applies to information submitted through the form.
  • Google Analytics -- collects anonymized usage data to help us understand site traffic.
  • Google Ads -- may be used to display advertising and measure campaign performance.
  • Font Awesome -- serves icon fonts via a CDN. No personal data is collected by this service.

How We Use Your Information

Information you submit through our audit request form is used to prepare and deliver your free visibility audit and to follow up with you about Paw & Prosper services. We will not add you to a marketing list without your consent or share your contact information with unrelated third parties.

Data Retention

We retain contact information submitted through our form for as long as needed to provide services or respond to your inquiry. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain uses of your data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
