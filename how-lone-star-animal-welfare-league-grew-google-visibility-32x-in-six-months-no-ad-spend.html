What It Actually Took to Get Here

  • Recovering a lapsed domain registration before it was lost for good, a three-month process resolved in July
  • Building Google Analytics and Search Console tracking from scratch
  • A full technical and on-page SEO pass across the site's main pages, including schema markup
  • Steady bilingual content: two press releases, four blog posts with Spanish translations, and ongoing updates as program numbers came in

Common Questions About This Case Study

How much did LSAWL's Google visibility grow?

Monthly Google search impressions grew about 32 times over between March and August 2026, from 1,574 to 51,098, with clicks growing roughly 4 times over the same period.

Did this require advertising spend?

No. LSAWL runs entirely on donations and volunteers. The growth came from SEO and AI-visibility work, not paid ads.

Can this work for other animal wellness businesses?

Yes. Paw & Prosper works with veterinary clinics, groomers, trainers, boarding facilities, pet brands, and animal welfare nonprofits on the same approach.
