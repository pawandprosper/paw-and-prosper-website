Common Questions from Shelters

Do you work with municipal shelters or only nonprofits?

Both. Whether you're a city-run animal control facility or a 501(c)(3) rescue nonprofit, the visibility challenges are similar. We tailor the strategy to your structure and goals.

How long until we see more adoptions from better SEO?

Most shelters see measurable improvements in search rankings and traffic within 3–4 months. Direct adoption impact depends on your current website, listing pages, and how people find animals. We look at the full picture, not just rankings.

Can you help us get more volunteers and donors, not just adopters?

Yes. Volunteer and donor acquisition often starts with the same search visibility that drives adoptions. We optimize for all three audiences, because they're all searching for you.

What makes you different from general digital marketing agencies?

We only work with animal wellness businesses and nonprofits. That means we understand shelter operations, the emotional stakes of adoption searches, and the challenges of competing with national organizations on a local budget.
