What's inside: the 20 checks

Four pillars, scored out of 100. Each check in the PDF includes the exact prompt or search to run, what good looks like, and the fix.

Pillar 1 · 20 points

Technical Foundation

Can Google and AI tools reach and read your website?

  1. Your site loads fast on a phoneMost people find you on their phone. Slow pages lose them before they ever see your services.
  2. Google has your important pagesIf Google hasn't indexed a page, neither Google Search nor its AI answers can show it.
  3. Your site tells machines who you areSchema markup is a hidden label on your site that spells out your business type, address, phone and hours for search engines.
  4. AI tools are allowed to read your siteA file called robots.txt can quietly block the crawlers AI search tools use to read your site.
Pillar 2 · 20 points

Authority Engine

Does your website prove you know your stuff?

  1. Every main service has its own pageOne “Services” page with a bullet list gives Google and AI nothing specific to match or quote.
  2. You have helpful guides, not just sales pagesAI tools recommend the businesses that explain things well. Guides show you're the expert.
  3. Something new or updated in the last 90 daysFresh, accurate pages tell people and machines you're open, active and current.
  4. Your pages link to each other and to bookingLinks between your pages help Google find everything and help visitors take the next step.
Pillar 3 · 20 points

AI & Search Optimization

When someone asks AI, are you the answer?

  1. AI names you when asked for the best near youThe core test: when a customer asks an AI assistant for a recommendation, are you in the answer?
  2. AI describes you correctlyBeing mentioned isn't enough. AI has to get your services, location and reputation right.
  3. Your first two sentences say what you do and whereAI tools quote short passages. Pages that answer up front are the easiest to quote.
  4. You have a real FAQPeople now type full questions into search and AI. A good FAQ answers them in your words.
Pillar 4 · 40 points

Local & Trust Signals

Do Google and AI trust you enough to recommend you?

  1. Your Google Business Profile is claimed and completeYour Google profile feeds Google Maps and Google's AI answers, and other AI tools read it too.
  2. Your Google Business Profile category is the right oneYour primary category tells Google which searches you're relevant for, and relevance is one of the three things Google says local results depend on.
  3. Your review count keeps up with the top 3Google says review count and rating factor into local ranking, and AI tools summarize your reviews for shoppers.
  4. A strong rating, with a review in the last 30 daysShoppers trust recent reviews, and so do the tools that summarize them.
  5. You reply to every reviewReplies show you're paying attention, and they add fresh, specific detail about your services.
  6. New photos and a post in the last 30 daysAn active profile looks open and trustworthy. A stale one looks closed.
  7. You're in the top 3 on the map for your main searchThe top 3 map results are what people see first on their phone, before they scroll or ask anything else.
  8. Trusted sites list you, with matching detailsBefore recommending you, AI tools cross-check what other sites say about you.
